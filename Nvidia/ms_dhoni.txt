Mahendra Singh Dhoni, commonly known as MS Dhoni, is a former Indian cricketer and captain of the Indian national team. 
He was born on 7 July 1981 in Ranchi, Jharkhand, India. Dhoni is widely regarded as one of the greatest wicket-keeper batsmen and captains in the history of cricket.

Dhoni made his One Day International (ODI) debut on 23 December 2004 against Bangladesh. 
He made his Test debut a year later on 2 December 2005 against Sri Lanka. 
His T20 International debut came on 1 December 2006 against South Africa.

Under his captaincy, India won several major tournaments:
- ICC T20 World Cup in 2007 (inaugural edition)
- ICC Cricket World Cup in 2011
- ICC Champions Trophy in 2013

Dhoni is known for his calm demeanor under pressure and finishing abilities. 
He has the nickname "Captain Cool". 
He is also remembered for hitting the winning six in the 2011 World Cup final against Sri Lanka.

Dhoni retired from Test cricket in December 2014. 
He stepped down as captain of limited-overs formats in January 2017, handing over to Virat Kohli. 
On 15 August 2020, he announced his retirement from all international cricket.

Apart from international cricket, Dhoni also had a highly successful career in the Indian Premier League (IPL) as the captain of Chennai Super Kings (CSK). 
Under his leadership, CSK won multiple IPL titles and became one of the most consistent teams in the tournament's history.

Beyond cricket, Dhoni has also served in the Indian Army as an honorary lieutenant colonel and is admired for his leadership qualities, humility, and contributions to Indian sports.
